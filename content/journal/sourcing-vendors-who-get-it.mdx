A vendor bench is the actual product of an event planning studio, even if nobody
sees it that way. The florals, the catering, the lighting — all of it depends on
relationships built years before a client ever calls.

## Skill isn't the only filter

Plenty of vendors are technically excellent and still wrong for a given event.
We're looking for something harder to test for: do they understand what a second
line is without being told, or a jumping broom, or why a repast is a celebration
and not just a meal afterward. That understanding shows up in a hundred small
decisions we'd otherwise have to specify ourselves.

## How we actually vet

We work with a vendor once on something low-stakes before we ever recommend them
for a client's biggest day. We ask how they handle a timeline slipping by twenty
minutes — the answer tells us more than any portfolio.

## The bench keeps growing

Every event adds someone new we trust, and occasionally removes someone we don't.
It's never finished, and that's the point — a static vendor list is a sign a
studio has stopped paying attention.
